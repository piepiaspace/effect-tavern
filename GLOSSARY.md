# Glossary

Canonical naming primitives (ubiquitous language) for Effect Tavern. Developers, coding agents, skills, and internal specifications must use these exact terms without substituting synonyms. Every entry binds a domain concept directly to its code identifier.

## Naming Principles

1. **Substance over ornament.** Dramaturgical and narratological terms (`diegetic`, `dramatic irony`, `has no agency`, `metagaming`, `bleedthrough`) are borrowed strictly for their orthogonal conceptual boundaries—disentangling mechanisms that legacy tavern ecosystems conflate—never as decorative literary jargon.
2. **Internal precision, surface plainness.**
   - **Internal (`packages/*/src`, prompts in `request.ts`, skills, specs):** Use exact, boundary-tight primitives (`diegetic`, `readout`, `chronicle`, `disclosure`, `slot`).
   - **Player & author UI (`locales/`):** Use plain, zero-friction words where everyday vocabulary is already unambiguous (`opening`, `prompt`, `tokens`, `persona`, `status`, `roll`, `tag`).

## 1. Fiction & Dramaturgy

- **`diegetic` / `non-diegetic`** — Whether content exists inside the story world, split three ways after film sound:
  - **`external diegetic`** — Inside the story world and perceptible to everyone in the scene (speech, visible action).
  - **`internal diegetic` (`thought`)** — Inside the story world, private to one mind. The reader sees it; other characters in the scene do not. Exists only when `persona` is present.
  - **`non-diegetic` (`note`)** — Outside the story world: out-of-character direction to the narrator or director on this turn.
- **`mark`** — Surface notation marking `thought` inside player text (such as `⦅⦆`, italics, or parentheses). A player-layer convention (e.g. a fragment and snippet in the starter writing pack); the wire protocol never hardcodes mark parsing.
- **`dramatic irony`** — An asymmetry between what the reader knows and what a character knows. `thought` asks the narrator for dramatic irony: counterparts remain ignorant of the thought, while narration may write around that gap.
- **`metagaming`** — A character acting on knowledge they cannot possess in-world (for example, an NPC replying to the player's unspoken `thought` or citing unrevealed lore).
- **`bleedthrough`** — The narrator speaking, acting, or deciding on the player's behalf (often called _impersonation_ or _godmodding_, split into _narrative impersonation_—describing what the player perceives—and _user impersonation_—writing the player's dialogue or choices).
  - _Contrast:_ Legacy tavern communities often lump both under "OOC / breaking character". `metagaming` is an in-world knowledge boundary violation; `bleedthrough` is a player-agency boundary violation.
- **`has no agency`** — A thought is not a move. The `decide` stage ignores `thought` when selecting mechanical actions.
- **`participant mode` / `author mode`** — Two positions on a coordinate pair rather than separate "character card" vs. "world simulator card" formats, determined by `persona` presence and default `input` channel:
  - **`participant mode`** (homodiegetic): `persona` is filled; the player inhabits a character and the model plays counterparts (`thought` applies).
  - **`author mode`** (heterodiegetic): `persona` is empty and `input` defaults to `"note"`; the player directs from outside the story while the model writes everyone.
- **`player`** — The real person at the keyboard. Purely non-diegetic: the word never appears on stage (dialogue, prose, notes, presence lists).
- **`player character`** — The one in the fiction the player speaks through, defined by agency, not narrative centrality (so not "protagonist"); author mode has none. Worn as `persona`; its proper-noun hole key is `user`.
  - _Contrast:_ `{{user}}` / `{{char}}` are ecosystem-compatible hole keys, not concept words.
- **`persona`** — Who the player is in the fiction. Both a `frame` slot (`persona`, filled by card default or left empty) and a named player library asset (`/personas`, where one may be marked the install-wide default). `local.persona` is tri-state (`undefined` follows the install default, `null` = none, `<id>` = explicit pick), snapshotted onto new stories (`story.persona`, changeable live on the next request without restarting). Participant cards inherit the install default in `yield` mode (`story.yields`: the card's own `persona` slot stays intact while the default's name fills `names.user` at lowest rank); author-mode cards never inherit it; an empty-text persona supplies `names.user` without blanking `persona`.
- **`hat`** — A named protocol shell (`/hats`, the player's) that primes a model family into continuation mode for a specific provider or endpoint—extracting the legitimate wire handshake that legacy tavern "jailbreak / prefill presets" buried inside style prompts. The routing wears it, not the model entry: `Routing.hat` is the main hat for every hat stage, `Routing.stageHats.<stage>` overrides it per stage (an id, or `null` for bare); the library puts the id on the stage's `Connection.hat` per request. Five slots: `narrate.lead` (first in the request, before `narrate` framing and `frame`), `narrate.head` (system messages after `frame`, `fiction`, and `<writing_guidelines>`, its handshake after every head system message, before the head breakpoint and `transcript`), `narrate.turn` (just before this turn's input), `narrate.tail` (at the very end of the request), and `review.head` (after `<world>` in the prose-reading stages `check`, `polish`, `record`); `lookup`, `decide`, and `direct` wear nothing. An entry is a role message (`system` / `user` / `assistant`, an assistant's optional `reasoning`) or a `tool` handshake (`toolName`, `toolInput`, the result `text`, optional `reasoning`), sent as an assistant `tool-call` plus its `tool-result` and needing native tools. `deliver: "tool"` (default `"text"`) has `narrate` hand its reply through one delivery tool call (`<name>({ text })`), decoded as it streams, with the delivery instruction in the lead; `toolChoice` is `"auto"` (the default) or `"force"`, and such a request may not end in an assistant prefill. Under tool delivery only, a hat may also carry **`tool`** (`{ name?, description?, param? }`): `name` is the delivery tool's identifier, the hat's alone (default `narrative`; it must start with a letter or `_` and hold at most 64 letters, digits, `_` and `-`, or the hat write is refused), while a non-blank `description` / `param` overrides the delivery tool's default description / `text` parameter description; and **`wrap: true`** (**tool wrap**, `hatWraps`, with either `toolChoice`): `messages[]` keeps only `narrate.lead` and `narrate.tail` (plus a built-in closing user message when neither holds one), and every other narrate message, as it would have been sent, goes into the delivery tool's description as role-marked sections (`<message role="…">`, a handshake as `tool_call="…"` / `tool_result="…"`; reasoning never), at its `{{context}}` hole or after its own wording. No message carries a cache breakpoint under wrap (the prefix cache is off), and an on-demand block (`demand`) wraps the same way. A hat is stored in one shape (`normalizeHatDelivery`): `toolChoice`, `tool` and `wrap` exist only with `deliver: "tool"`. Empty by default.
- **`slop` (`claudeism`)** — Over-repeated modelisms—stock words, formulaic antitheses (`not X, but Y`), melodrama, or clichés—that self-reinforce once admitted into openings, examples, or history.

## 2. Runtime, Turn & State

### Turn Spine & Execution

- **`Turn`** — One complete execution pass triggered by player input, running a fixed stage pipeline rather than a single monolithic chat completion:
  `lookup → decide → settle → gate → direct → narrate → check → polish → record`.
  Structured stages (`lookup`, `decide`, `direct`, `check`, `polish`) are opt-in and activate automatically from what the card declares; a pure chat card runs only `narrate` and `record`.
  - _Contrast:_ Legacy tavern clients force one completion to simultaneously roleplay, track variables in `<status>` XML/JSON blocks, and propose options—degrading prose quality and coupling state to fragile regex scraping.
- **`Stage` (`Phase`)** — A bounded harness invocation with its own assembled request (`request.ts`) and tool/schema contract. The model never owns the outer loop.
  - **`lookup`** — Pre-narration context retrieval over unlocked `lore` entries, lore `sections`, pack `entries`, and `recall` product rows (`context.ts`). The host builds one `catalog` view per turn: when complete (`≤ 256` units and `≤ 10,000` chars) and a `DecisionModel` is enabled, a single probability pass (`p > 0.5`, top 8) settles `lookup` with zero LLM calls; otherwise `lookup` runs the LLM `search_context` / `read_context` tool loop seeded with the `Available` catalog block, freezing `deliveries`.
  - **`decide`** — Single-beat action selection against `rules.actions`. The model outputs an action ID (plus a probability when `roll` is `{ judge }`); dice and outcomes are computed by code.
  - **`settle`** — Deterministic rule evaluation (`card.ts`). Applies `hit`, `miss`, and `do` effects using seeded RNG, clamping scalar fields to bounds.
  - **`gate`** — Evaluates `lore` `unlock` floors and `offer` windows against post-settlement `WorldState` and turn events.
  - **`direct`** — Opt-in (`card.director: true`) stage that runs when `gate` opens windows, reading the factual ledger to partition open lore into `must` vs. `may` and emit a one-line scene `tone` (`Brief`).
  - **`narrate`** — Streaming prose generation. The only stage that reads the full `frame` (including writing slots `pov`, `verbosity`, `style`), `fiction`, and the `transcript`. Prose is non-authoritative over mechanical state, and `narrate` never sees raw scalar numbers.
  - **`check`** — Post-narration verification of `guard` prohibitions and `must` disclosures. When a `DecisionModel` is enabled, a fast probability pass (`decisionPass`) settles clear-cut turns first (`guard` `< 0.25`, open lore `< 0.25` or `> 0.75`, `must` `> 0.75`) without ever triggering a rewrite itself; ambiguous or failed readings fall through to the LLM `check`, which triggers at most one correction rewrite.
  - **`polish`** — Post-check prose pass triggered only when `lexicon` patterns match narration outside dialogue; rewrites only matched sentences (`switches.polish: false` disables it).
  - **`record`** — Post-prose structured extraction. Answers active `readouts`, emits `chronicle` and `theater` products, and commits sparse `world` `DomainChange` operations.

### Tree, Projections & Compression

- **`Log`** (`log.ts`) — Generic immutable append/amend tree shared across `runtime`, `server`, and `client`. Every write (`add`, `amend`, `checkout`, `drop`) produces a `Change` value applied via `apply`. **`head`** is the single active cursor (a leaf node ID, or `null` on an empty thread); the active reading path is the root-to-`head` chain.
  - _Contrast:_ Replaces flat chat arrays with per-message `swipes[]` and duplicated "branch checkpoint" chat files. Branching at any historical turn is a zero-copy tree operation.
- **`Node` (`Turn<Beat>`)** (`journal.ts`, `derived.ts`) — One node in the `Log`: player `input` (`{ text, note?, invoke? }`) plus recorded model outputs, RNG seeds (`beats`), `deliveries`, `answers`, `products`, `changes`, `adjust`, and `settlementStatus`. Derived state snapshots are never stored on nodes.
- **`sibling`** — Nodes sharing the same `parent`, split into three exact stage restarts rather than a single ambiguous "swipe / reroll":
  - **`swipe`** — Re-runs the turn from `lookup` (new retrieval, new roll, new prose).
  - **`reword`** — Re-runs from `narrate` (preserving frozen `deliveries` and settled `beats`), optionally appending a one-turn `note`.
  - **`edit`** — Replaces prose (`text`), revises `input` while keeping prose, or both, re-running only `check` and `record` so state re-settles cleanly.
- **`Derived`** (`derived.ts`) — All state computed purely by folding a root-to-node path: scalar `fields`, `world` entities/records, `lore` floors and counters (`since`, `shown`), compression boundaries, `Known:` / `Current:` lines, and client projections (`TimelineNode`, `TurnDetail`, `ActiveContextView`). Rewinding or switching branches never desynchronizes state.
- **`WorldState` & `DomainChange`** (`world.ts`) — Unified state object `{ fields, world, lore }` read by `Cond`, `heading`, and prompt assembly. `world` products mutate exclusively through recorded `DomainChange` operations (`insert_entity`, `patch_entity`, `remove_entity`, `patch_record`, `replace`) under two-phase settlement (`settlementStatus`: `pending` | `committed` | `failed` | `skipped`, recoverable via `retryRecord` / `skipRecord`).
- **`Fold`** — Two pure path reductions:
  1. **Product `fold`** (`product.ts`): how `chronicle` and `theater` values accumulate along a path—`"latest"`, `"append"`, or `{ key }` (table row merge by key column).
  2. **Context compression** (`history.ts`): folding older turns' `notes` and disclosed lore over fixed node intervals into a cached prefix block. Unlike rolling chat-summarizer plugins that rewrite a depth-injected summary block and invalidate KV cache, interval folds keep prompt breakpoints byte-identical across turns and branches.
- **`Transcript`** — The chronological dialogue view rendered from path nodes for `narrate` only: past `act` inputs, assistant `prose`, and disclosed lore. Never stored, and never spliced with out-of-band system prompts.
- **`Head` / `Tail` & `<world>`** — The only two prompt assembly zones (`request.ts`). In `narrate`, **`head`** holds the hat's `narrate.lead`, harness stage framing, `<world>` (non-writing `frame` slots: `<persona>`, `<setting>`, etc.), `<fiction>` bands, `<writing_guidelines>` (writing slots `pov`, `verbosity`, `voice`, `style`, `language` and player-added head fragments), and the hat's `narrate.head` as a stable cache prefix (folded history sits in `<story_summary>`), while **`tail`** sits after `transcript` and current input, carrying `<recalled_context>` (delivered entries), `<turn_state>` (outcomes, `Current:`), `<stage_directions>` (`MUST`/`MAY reveal`, `Brief` tone, `note`), `<correction>`, and `<writing_tail>` (player tail fragments). Off-stage passes (`lookup`, `decide`, `direct`, `check`, `polish`, `record`) never read `fiction` or writing directives: their cached head holds harness stage framing plus **`<world>`** (`layer.ts` `worldOf`—card-declared frame slots and `persona`, excluding `pov`, `verbosity`, `voice`, `style`, `language`, and player-added fragments), and their user message wraps reference text in XML (`<input>`, `<draft>`, `<scene>`) before the stage's task instruction. Cache breakpoints sit at the end of `head`, the folded prefix, and the last prior history turn; under a hat's tool wrap there are none, and every zone but the hat's `narrate.lead` and `narrate.tail` is carried in the delivery tool's description instead of `messages[]`.
  - _Negative constraint:_ Numeric chat-depth injection (`@D0`, `@D4`, `Author's Note`, `post_history_instructions`) does not exist. Splicing dynamic text `N` messages from the end of history mutates the message prefix on every turn, destroying provider KV prefix caching. Placing turn-level guidance in `tail` achieves end-of-context recency weight with 100% prefix cache preservation.

### Input, Layers & Customization

- **`Channel`** — Which route player input takes: `"act"` (enters the `transcript`) or `"note"` (feeds `direct` and `narrate` on this turn only, never entering history). Cards declare default `input: "act" | "note"`, and `Input.invoke` (`string[]`) pins player-accessible context resources for the turn.
  - _Negative constraint:_ Never embed `[OOC: ...]` brackets in `act` messages or maintain sticky `Author's Note` depth prompts; non-diegetic direction belongs on `note`.
- **`Fragment`**, **`Slot`** & **`Band`** (`fragment.ts`) — Unit of prompt assembly (`{ id, text?, label?, role?, enabled?, when?, mode?, at? }`).
  - **`Slot`**: A `frame` fragment ID where player layers and the card meet. Known slots (`SLOTS`): `persona`, `pov`, `voice`, `style`, `setting`, `language`, `verbosity`.
  - **`Band`**: A `fiction` fragment gated by `when` over a field or state threshold; bands on one axis represent mutually exclusive narrative postures.
- **`Layer`, `Local` & `Pack`** (`layer.ts`) — Uniform customization row resolved in one strict rank order:
  `defaultPack` (the install's starred default pack, unless mounted on this card) → `card` → mounted `packs` (`local.mounts` order) → `local` (`/cards/<id>/local`) → `persona`.
  The harness itself is neutral and ships no default fragments, products, snippets, format keys, or lexicon; a fresh library seeds one ordinary first-party **starter pack** (`starter-pack.ts`) as its `defaultPack`, which the player may edit, unstar, or delete. Higher rank wins by `id`, except `mode: "yield"` (defers if already filled) and `mode: "both"` (concatenates). Player rows are locked at decode (`locked`): they cannot carry `when` or `role` on fragments or alter a card-declared product's `shape`/`role` (over another player pack's product, a valid whole declaration may replace its structure); invalid items land in `Resolved.dropped` without throwing. A **`Pack`** (`/packs`, ecosystem _enhancer_ / _text LoRA_) bundles standing directives (`fragments`), `switches`, products/product patches, Skill-style reference `entries` (`<pack>/<entry>`, carrying optional explicit `title` / `summary` front matter and up to 8 on-demand `sections`), `lexicon`, `snippets`, `format`, and `names`.
  - _Negative constraint:_ Monolithic **presets** do not exist; prompt assembly, style layers, continuation hats, and endpoint connections are strictly separated by owner.
- **`Writing` (`switches`)** — Bounded player controls (`pov`, `echo`, `agency`, `verbosity`, `polish`) compiled into `pov` and `verbosity` frame slots via `SWITCH_SENTENCES` (`layer.ts`). Compiled switches always yield to explicit card or free-text slot fragments.
- **`Names`** (`names.ts`) — Single-pass proper-noun dictionary replacing `{{key}}` holes (`user`, `char`, or custom keys) across fragments, products, snippets, and greetings. Values are single-line strings containing no `{{` (ruling out recursion by construction). No key has a fallback value, `user` included. A hole nothing fills in the card's own text stays there as the literal `{{key}}` and goes out: nothing of the card's is dropped or refused for it, and the player sees the literal and may bind a name or persona. One in a player row's entry (pack, local, a hat) leaves that entry out, listed in the trace's `omitted` (`unfilled_hole`), not `Resolved.dropped` (the locks' refusals). The rule lives in one place, `expand`'s author argument. Two holes the harness fills itself, never from the table (`HARNESS_HOLES`): **`{{tool}}`**, the delivery tool's name in force, in delivery instructions, the hat's `tool.description` / `tool.param` and its narrate entries, whenever the hat delivers by tool (over a Names key of that spelling); and **`{{context}}`**, the serialized story context, only in the delivery tool's description and only under the hat's wrap (the first one is replaced; without one the context follows the wording after a blank line). Outside those, either is an ordinary hole under the rule above.
  - _Negative constraint:_ No imperative template macros (`{{setvar}}`, `{{getvar}}`, `{{roll}}`, `{{random}}`, EJS, or STscript).
- **`Snippet` vs. `Format`** — **`Snippet`** (`{ name, text, after?, hint?, note? }`) inserts play-meaningful templates (`thought` marks, `note` toggles, narrator directions) from the `/` or `+` menu without auto-sending. **`Format`** (`{ label, text, after? }`) provides pure punctuation/bracket keys (`「」`, `『』`) above a focused input.

### Card Mechanics, Lore & Products

- **`Shelf`** (`server/src/shelf.ts`) — The install's cards, all of them: one table, one owner per row. The program ships none and reads none from beside itself. A card enters through `install` (a card file's YAML/JSON text or a value, through the whole gate `validateCard`; an id already on the shelf is refused until `replace`), leaves through `remove` (refused while it has stories), and is a file again through `text` (`install(text(id))` is the same card). The workshop writes it directly, with no draft beside a card: `create` is a blank card of a fresh id (every section empty passes the gate, and a story of it begins on the player's first line), `whole` is the card with its revision (a digest of it as stored), and `save` the card whole over the one of its id through the same gate, from the revision it was read at: a card that changed meanwhile is `card-changed` and stands.
  - _Negative constraint:_ No **template**, **built-in** or **bundled** card: every card on the shelf is the player's copy, including one that came from the project's own examples.
- **`Manuscript`** (`client/src/manuscript.ts`) — A card as the workshop's page shows it: three kinds of plain text. A **chapter** is a `fiction` fragment with no `when` (always in the head; an optional `label` over prose), an **opening** is a greeting's shown `text`, an **entry** is a `lore` entry of one layer (its name is its `id`, its first sentence its hook). `manuscriptOf(card)` reads it and `cardOf(card, manuscript)` writes it back into the card it was read from: what it does not show (a `Band`, a note-only greeting, a layered entry, `rules`, `guard`, every other key) stays where it was, a value the author did not touch is written as it was read, and a block with no text is not written.
  - _Contrast:_ Not a second card format and not a draft: nothing is stored but the `Card` on the `Shelf`. A lore entry's `sections` are its on-demand appendices and are not chapters.
- **`Origin`** (`runtime/src/library.ts`) — How a card reached this install, stored beside it on the shelf row: `{ via: "file" | "link" | "workshop" | "depot", url?, depot?, version?, edited? }`. The install's fact, distinct from the author's own `creator` / `version` / `source` inside the `Card`. `edited` marks a card that arrived from elsewhere and was since changed through the workshop.
- **`Depot`** (card library, 卡库; `server/src/depot.ts`) — An optional place cards are kept that the install does not own: a directory or `https` URL holding `index.json` (`{ name, entries: [{ id, title, file, summary?, creator?, version?, type? }] }`) and the card files it names. Read-only and never read on its own: the install knows a depot only after the player adds it, and `pull` copies one card onto the `Shelf` (an update is the same pull with `replace`).
  - _Negative constraint:_ Not the **library** (the install's own SQLite store, `data.ts`), and never a live source of a story's card: a story reads the shelf's copy alone.

- **`Card`** (`card.ts`) — Single declarative schema (`decodeCard`) encompassing metadata, `frame`, `fiction`, `greetings`, `passages`, `rules`, `lore`, `products`, `catalogs`, `views`, `guard`, `lexicon`, `names`, `director`, `input`, and `heading`.
  - _Negative constraint:_ Not a "character card". Fixed single-NPC schemas (`description`, `personality`, `scenario`, `mes_example`, `post_history_instructions`) force cast, RPG, and world-simulation authors to abuse character fields as prompt scripts; in `Card`, whether `fiction` depicts one character, an ensemble, or an entire world is content, not schema.
- **`Greeting`** — Root opening (`greetings[n]: { text?, do?, note? }`). `do` settles initial branch effects before turn 1; `note` (or a player `--note` / `Field.start` override on `greet`) triggers a dynamic narrated opening on node 0.
- **`Passage` & `Boundary`** (`card.ts`, `session.ts`, `history.ts`) — Post-`record` state-gated transition (`passages[n]: { when, text?, note?, do?, digest?, end? }`) evaluated first-match-wins and non-reentrantly after a player turn's `record` commits. Appends a static (`text`) or dynamically narrated (`note`) transition child node (`passage: true`, recoverable via `passageStatus`). Declaring `digest` (`string[]` of `record`-stage product IDs) runs an interval `record` pass over the ending epoch's transcript and marks **`boundary: true`** on the transition node—slicing `narratorHistory`, `ledger`, `Known:`, prefix folds, and `window` lore residency cleanly from the new epoch while keeping scalar `fields`, `lore` unlock floors, and `world`/`chronicle` state folded over the full root-to-node path. Setting `end: true` (or satisfying `rules.over` post-`record`) marks `end: "over"` and guards the terminal leaf against appending child turns.
- **`Field` & `Drawn`** (`card.ts`) — Typed scalar state (`int`, `number`, `boolean`, `enum`) with `initial`, optional `min`/`max`/`options`, `hidden` (omitted from UI), `start` (configurable at story start), and `view` override (`"gauge" | "scale"`). `drawn()` derives UI presentation from field shape and band thresholds (`steps`, `gauge`, `scale`, or `value`).
- **`Cond`** (`cond.ts`) — Closed boolean expression tree (`is`, `lt | lte | gt | gte`, `all`, `any`, `not`, `{ event }`) over `fields.<path>` (or bare `<path>`), `world.<product>.<key>`, and `lore.<id>.since | shown`. The only logic available to card authors.
- **`Effect`** (`Effect_`) — Closed mutation vocabulary: `{ path, set }`, `{ path, add }`, and `{ event }` (with `{path}` post-effect interpolation).
- **`Action`, `Chance` & `Beat`** — Mechanical move in `rules.actions` gated by `when`, with optional `roll` (`Chance`: fixed number, `{ path }`, or `{ judge }` answered in `decide`) and `hit` / `miss` / `do` effects. Each resolved roll records a **`Beat`** (`action`, `seed`, `chance?`) on the node.
- **`Readout`** — Post-prose multiple-choice judgement in `rules.readouts` asked during `record` when `when` holds. The model chooses an option ID; code applies its `do` effects. Fields writable only by `actions` are purely mechanical; fields that prose may move must attach a `Readout`.
- **`Lore`, `Hook`, `Lore Layer`, `Section` & `ContextPolicy`** (`lore.ts`, `context.ts`):
  - **`Lore`**: Progressive knowledge entry (`{ id, layers, sections?, unlock, offer, ...ContextPolicy }`).
  - **`Hook`**: The first sentence of `layers[0]` (or of a `section`'s text), shown in the `lookup` index.
  - **`Lore Layer`**: Cumulative depth (`1..n`) gated by **`unlock`** (monotone floor along the path) and **`offer`** (per-turn window condition reading state, events, and `lore.<id>.since | shown`).
  - **`Section`**: Ungated on-demand appendix under a card `lore` entry or pack reference `entry` (`sections: [{ id, title?, text }]`, addressed as `card:<entry>#<section>` or `pack:<pack>/<entry>#<section>`), readable by `lookup` while the parent entry is visible without altering layer progression.
  - **`ContextPolicy`**: `{ access?: { model, player }, activation?: "always" | "on_demand", residency?: "permanent" | "window" | "turn" }` governing who can invoke an unlocked resource, whether it auto-injects or waits for lookup/pin, and how long a **`Disclosure`** stays resident.
  - _Negative constraint:_ Replaces legacy **worldbooks / lorebooks** and substring/regex **keyword triggers** (`constant` blue-light vs. `selective` green-light, `scan_depth`, `insertion_order`, recursive keyword chains), which misfire on passing mentions, miss synonyms, and cannot model monotone plot progression.
- **`Product` & `Role`** (`product.ts`) — Typed data (`text`, `list`, `record`, `table`) produced by `record` and declared with an explicit `role`:
  - **`world`** — Mutable current-state records/tables (`scene`, `people`, `bag`) living in `WorldState.world` and updated via sparse `DomainChange` ops during `record`. Supports `feed: true` (`Current:` line) and `recall` (lookup index).
  - **`chronicle`** — Append/folded narrative memory along the path (such as default `notes`, whose `feed` supplies `Known:` and prefix compression). Shown in Inspector.
  - **`theater`** — Player-only side content (`inner`, `options`, commentary), never fed back or recalled (`feed` and `recall` forbidden). Generated by independent `record` calls, triggered every `turn` or on player `demand`, with `surface: "theater"` (default, drawn beneath the turn) or `surface: "composer"` (`list` shape offered above the input box, never `demand`).
- **`Ledger`** — Per-turn delta summary shown under a turn's prose pill: field deltas, beats/rolls, disclosures, and `world` product changes.
- **`ViewShell`** (`view-shell.ts`, `view-template.ts`, `view-bindings.ts`, `Card.views`) — Shadow-DOM in-stream presentation shell (`{ id, label?, consumes?, residency?, when?, media?, html, css? }`) compiled on the host into a scriptless, URL-free template AST (`compileView`) over five declarative directives (`data-text`, `data-if` / `data-unless`, `data-each` + `data-as`, `data-media`, `data-attr-*` / `data-var-*`) plus `<details data-open="latest">` and `:host([data-latest])`. A card shell reads `{ fields, world, theater, media, names }` (plus `value` and record-key aliases when `consumes` names a single product) and may claim zero or more `theater` / `composer` products via `consumes: string[]` (`consumableSurface`), suppressing duplicate default `<Theater>` / `<Options>` output on every node where the view applies (no `when`, or its `when` held there: `applicableViews`), mounted there or not, so a `latest` view's products draw no default box on past turns; where its `when` does not hold they fall back to the default. `residency` defaults to `"turn"` when `consumes` is non-empty and `"latest"` otherwise. A card may claim each product only once across its views.

- **`Catalog`** (`product.ts`) — Static attachment registry on a card (`catalogs: [{ id, baseUrl?, items: [{ id, when?, variants? }] }]`) referenced by an `enum` column (`catalog: "<id>"`) on `chronicle` or `theater` products. Each `record` call restricts valid enum options to items whose `when` holds; readers resolve `variants` deterministically from the node's seed.
- **`Guard` & `Lexicon`** — **`Guard`**: post-prose prohibitions (`guard: string[]`) verified in `check` and never placed in `narrate` prompts. **`Lexicon`**: wildcard prose patterns (`{ pattern, hint? }`) rewritten outside dialogue in `polish`.
  - _Negative constraint:_ Never stuff negative prohibition walls ("DO NOT act for `{{user}}`", "NEVER write phrase X") into `narrate` prompts; negative imperatives degrade voice and induce ironic-process violations.

### Session & Host Boundary

- **`Session`** (`session.ts`) — In-memory runtime binding one `Card`, resolved `Layer` rows, stage `Connection` bindings (plus optional `DecisionModel`), and a `Log` `Store`.
- **`Action` (Session Wire Protocol)** — Closed union accepted by `perform`: `act`, `greet`, `checkout`, `swipe`, `reword`, `edit`, `setField`, `demand`, `retryRecord`, `skipRecord`.
- **`Event`** — Streamed items emitted by `perform`: `stage` (phase started), `delta` (streaming `narrate` prose/reasoning), `call` (completed LLM or `via: "decision"` call metadata/trace), `exchange` (one HTTP request or its result, observed by the server), and `change` (committed `Log` mutation + projected node).
- **`Stop`** — Stream cancellation split by progress: before the first prose character, aborts cleanly leaving no node; after the first prose character, halts `narrate`, skips `check`, runs `record`, and commits the partial node with `end: "stopped"`.
- **`Connection`** (`provider/src/providers.ts`) — Language-model endpoint configuration (`anthropic`, `openai-compatible`, `gemini`), model ID, capabilities (`prefill`, `reasoningBack`), cache TTL, reasoning effort, token limits, proxy setting, and the `hat` id the routing puts on it for the stage (a model entry names none). The harness sends zero hardcoded sampling parameters (`temperature`, `topP`, etc.).
- **`DecisionModel` (`DecisionConfig`, `decisionPass`)** (`provider/src/decision.ts`, `runtime/src/decision.ts`) — Second model class beside language-model `Connection`s (`google` Gemini Decisions, `openai` Decisions, `typesafe` Jev), taking one state document and typed questions to return probabilities with zero prose generation. Configured once per install (`/decision`, toggled via `pre-check`) to settle complete-catalog `lookup` (`p > 0.5`, top 8) and clear-cut `check` passes (`LO = 0.25`, `HI = 0.75`), falling back cleanly to the stage's language model on incomplete catalogs, ambiguous probabilities, or errors.
- **`Fault`** — A failed call or operation, with its source and the facts available there. Local refusals carry a known code, upstream failures carry the original provider or transport error, and internal failures carry diagnostics for the operator.
- **`Logbook`** — The running server’s account of a story’s turns and individual model requests. It shows exchanges as they happen and keeps turn summaries and failed attempts for later inspection. Players see the word 日志 / log.
  - _Contrast:_ Not the turn's `Ledger` (the delta summary under a turn's prose pill), nor `Log` (the tree), `journal`, `record` or the Inspector's `trace`.

## 3. Legacy Tavern Concepts & Negative Constraints

When reading external cards, writing prompts, or designing features, map legacy tavern concepts onto Effect Tavern's orthogonal primitives and enforce their negative constraints:

| Legacy Tavern Concept                              | Effect Tavern Primitive(s)                                                             | Architectural Reason & Negative Constraint                                                                                                                      |
| -------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **`preset`** (monolithic prompt + sampler bundle)  | `request.ts` + `Layer` / `Pack` / `switches` + `Hat` + `Connection`                    | Presets conflate wire roles, jailbreaks/prefills, player prose style, status-bar prompts, and endpoint parameters into one brittle file.                        |
| **`character card`** (`chara_card_v2` / `ccv3`)    | `Card` (`frame` + `fiction` + `participant` / `author` coordinates)                    | Fixed single-NPC fields (`description`, `personality`, `mes_example`, `post_history_instructions`) distort cast, RPG, and protagonist-free world cards.         |
| **`depth` / `@D` injection & `Author's Note`**     | `at: "head" \| "tail"` + `note` (`Channel`)                                            | Splicing prompts `N` messages from the end of chat mutates the historical prefix every turn, invalidating KV prefix cache and polluting `transcript`.           |
| **`worldbook` / `lorebook` & keyword triggers**    | `Lore` (`unlock`, `offer`, `sections`) + `lookup` (`hook`) + `ContextPolicy`           | Substring/regex chat scanning (`constant` blue-light vs. `selective` green-light, `scan_depth`) misfires on passing mentions and lacks monotone plot staging.   |
| **Inline status bars, MVU blocks & regex scripts** | `decide` / `settle` + `record` (`Readout`, `Product`, `DomainChange`) + `ViewShell`    | Asking `narrate` to emit `<status>` XML/JSON or variable patches and scraping them with regex corrupts prose voice and desynchronizes state (`inline-product`). |
| **`[OOC: ...]` inline chat brackets**              | `note` (`Channel`) vs. `thought` (`internal diegetic` `mark`)                          | Inline `[OOC]` pollutes permanent `transcript` history and conflates non-diegetic direction with private character monologue.                                   |
| **Negative prompt walls ("DO NOT...")**            | `switches.agency` (`pov` slot) + `Guard` (`check`) + `Lexicon` (`polish`)              | Narrator-facing prohibition lists bloat prompts and trigger ironic-process violations; verification and slop rewriting belong post-prose.                       |
| **Stateful macros (`{{setvar}}`, `{{roll}}`)**     | `Cond` + `Effect` + `Action.roll` + single-pass `Names` (`{{key}}`)                    | Imperative template scripting hides untestable state inside prose strings (`unsupported-macro`).                                                                |
| **Flat chat `swipes[]` & branch checkpoint files** | `Log` (`Node`, `head`, `checkout`) + `sibling` (`swipe`, `reword`, `edit`) + `Derived` | Linear chat arrays duplicate files on mid-thread branches and desynchronize external variable stores; tree path folding keeps state exact everywhere.           |
